What Twilio will charge you

Crixin doesn't take a cut. The numbers below are the standard published Twilio rates as of mid-2026 for outbound calls placed from a US Twilio number. Always verify on the live Twilio pricing page — these change.

DestinationOutbound voiceSMS (per message)
United States · Canada~$0.014 / minute~$0.0083
Egypt~$0.135 / minute (mobile)~$0.0588
UAE~$0.155 / minute (mobile)~$0.078
UK~$0.025 / minute (mobile)~$0.0461
Spain~$0.075 / minute (mobile)~$0.0813

Rates are mobile-side; landline rates are usually 30–60% lower. Twilio also charges ~$1/month for the phone number itself, and Deepgram (optional, only for transcribe_call) prices at roughly $0.0043/minute on the Nova-2 tier. A 60-second call to Egypt with a transcription comes out to ~$0.14 total. A 30-second domestic US call with no transcription is < $0.01.
