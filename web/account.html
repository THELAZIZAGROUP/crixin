No Voice Wrapped synced yet. Run the CLI locally and pipe your Wrapped output here — your transcripts stay on your laptop, only the aggregate numbers land in your account.

npm i -g crixin
crixin voice ingest                                    # pull Twilio + Deepgram
crixin voice wrapped --year 2026 --json | curl -X POST \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d @- \
  --cookie "$(grep crixin_session ~/.crixin/cookies.txt)" \
  https://crixin.com/api/voice/wrapped

A first-class crixin voice wrapped --sync flag ships with the hosted Pro tier. Until then, the curl above works from any signed-in browser session.
